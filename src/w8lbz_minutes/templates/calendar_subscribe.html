Add the club calendar to the calendar app you already use, and it stays up to date by itself.

Your own link

The club events, chow nights and the birthdays members share, from a month ago to a year ahead. Keep this link to yourself: anyone who has it can see what you see here.

  • iPhone or iPad: Settings → Calendar → Accounts → Add Account → Other → Add Subscribed Calendar, and paste the link. Or open it here.
  • Google Calendar (on a computer): Other calendars → + → From URL, and paste the link. Google only checks it every few hours.
  • Outlook: Add calendar → Subscribe from web, and paste the link.
