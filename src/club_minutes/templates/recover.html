Recover admin access

For when the club can't reach its admins, or an admin is working against the club. The officers can make a member an admin, and can suspend admins:

  1. An officer starts it. More than half of the officers (at least 2) approve.
  2. The member named accepts.
  3. After {{ wait }} days it's done. If an admin objects: two-thirds of the officers, and {{ wait_objected }} days.

Every member is emailed at each step. If it isn't approved and accepted within {{ lapse }} days, it ends by itself.
